WHAT TO WATCH / EDITORIAL CUES

Win the next possession.

Close out to the shooters. East Texas made 12 threes against FDU; Dodd and Carrio combined for nine.

Finish the stop. FDU won the rebound battle 46–36. Watch whether Portland can turn its size into clean defensive rebounds.

Value the ball. Portland's UT Arlington loss included 21 turnovers. Look for calm outlets and quick decisions under pressure.

04 / THE BIGGER PICTURE

A team with a world of stories.

Basketball on The Bluff.

Portland competes in the West Coast Conference. This is Shantay Legans' sixth season, with a roster built from returning players and a new group of transfers.

The Pilots are riverboat pilots: the name connects to the campus above the Willamette River. Look for the ship's wheel, not airplane wings.

“We love shooting a lot of 3's.”Shantay Legans · Nov. 2, 2026 · Kyle Garcia / Portland Athletics ↗
