The Setlist

Import chord sheets

Drop PDFs here, or

Text-based sheets become interactive charts (chords, lyrics, progressions). Scanned pages and big songbooks are kept as the original PDF, and you can type in the progression yourself. The folder name becomes the artist (your Wilco folder gets filed under Wilco), and "(capo 3)" in a file name sets the capo.

Paste a tab

From the Ultimate Guitar app or website: open the tab, select and copy its text, then paste it here. You can also save just the link and title for now; the "Open in Ultimate Guitar" button will take you back to it.

Backup & move between devices

Your songs live only in this browser on this device. To move them to the iPad (or keep a copy), save a backup file, AirDrop it over, and load it there.
