Your 7 noodles

Every noodle has 5 balls and a letter, just like in the guide. Noodles can be flipped and turned any way.

How 2D challenges work

Open the case to the flat board. Put the colored noodles exactly where the picture shows, then fill every gray hole using the noodles listed on the card.

How 3D challenges work

Close the case to use the triangle board. The pictures go from the top (1 ball) down to the bottom (15 balls), just like the guide. Build from the bottom up. Noodles can lie flat or stand on a slant.

Stuck?

Hint shows one missing noodle at a time with a dashed ring. Answer shows them all. Tap Solved to keep score on this device.
