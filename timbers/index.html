THE SEASON / NOVEMBER 15, 2026

The next movement.

A new coaching chapter. A familiar city. Eleven players learning to move as one.

At Providence Park, the ball is only part of the story. Watch the player who creates the passing lane, the defender who steps forward while his partner covers, or the goalkeeper who makes an extra option behind the press. This guide begins with faces and numbers, then asks a second question: what is each person helping the team do?

Portland’s coaching picture changed during 2026. The club parted ways with Phil Neville, used Jack Cassidy as interim head coach, and appointed Martí Cifuentes in July. His route through Scandinavian and English soccer gives the new chapter an international frame. The useful matchday question is how his ideas become recognizable habits.

The immediate test is FC Dallas in the playoffs. The club has announced an away opener on Nov. 21 and a home match on Nov. 28. The official series announcement explains the best-of-three format. Those dates remain visible when the statistics provider has not yet added the postseason.

Start with the first pass. When a center back receives, do the midfielders show for the ball at different heights? Does a fullback move wide, stay deep or advance? Those movements change the next player’s options. A formation label gives you a starting picture; the distances between players explain whether the picture works.

Next, follow the moment possession changes. A team can try to win the ball back immediately or retreat into a compact shape. Neither response succeeds by itself. The nearest player needs help behind him. Pick one midfielder and watch three defensive transitions without following the ball the whole way.

Finally, compare the group before and after a substitution. Does a new forward offer runs behind the defense? Does another midfielder make the buildup calmer? A substitute changes relationships as well as personnel. Use the pitch to record what you actually see, and keep your observations separate from the published match statistics.
