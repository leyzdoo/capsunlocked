Math secret: colors are multiplication!

In Prime Climb, a number’s colors tell you its prime ingredients. Two orange slices make 4 = 2 × 2. Orange + purple + green make 42 = 2 × 3 × 7. To divide, remove the matching ingredients.

Classic rules · quick reference

  1. Roll two 10-sided dice (0 means 10). Doubles give FOUR uses of the same number.
  2. Move by adding, subtracting, multiplying, or dividing, applying each die separately. Use every roll unless you win. Stay on whole numbers from 0–101.
  3. Bump any pawn you finish on (even your own) back to 0. Passing through doesn't bump.
  4. Draw one physical Prime Card if an eligible pawn ends on an all-red prime number above 10 that it wasn't on at the start of the turn.

First player to reach 101 exactly with both pawns wins. One pawn at 101 is retired; the remaining dice go to the other pawn. No bouncing past 101.

Pick your adventure

🟢 Junior mode

Math for Love published an ages-6+ variation: addition/subtraction only, no cards. Bumps send pawns back 10, not to Start. A red prime landing triggers a choice: advance that pawn to the next red prime, or double the other pawn if possible.

⚡ Sprint mode

Classic movement rules, but the first pawn to 101 wins. Great for short rounds.

🧩 Helpful family tweaks

Try predicting a CPU move before revealing it, talking aloud about two choices, or pausing to decode factor colors.
