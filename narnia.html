Chapter III

A Warm Hearth

Gas fireplace • Fireplace Xtrordinair 564 GSR2
  1. Use the fireplace remote's power button.
  2. The thermometer button cycles through manual (thermostat OFF), ON and SMART thermostat modes.
  3. Use the arrows to adjust the target temperature in ON or SMART mode.
  4. In manual mode, select the flame icon using MODE and adjust with arrows. The fan and accent light have separate modes.
  5. Use the power button to switch off when finished.

Manual pp. 12–15. A beep normally acknowledges commands; thermostat target changes do not beep. Do not alter pilot or battery receiver settings.

Hot glass causes burns. Keep children supervised, keep flammable items away and never remove the protective barrier. If the burner fails to light, stop using it and contact Guest Services.

Watch the GreenSmart 2 fireplace remote tutorial
Heating and cooling

Keep all connected mini-split heads in the same mode (heat or cooling). Cadet wall heaters provide heat only. Exact remote instructions pending.

Bedrooms — beware the king's bed!

The king bed frame extends beyond the mattress. Give yourself extra room when walking around it, especially at night. Ceiling fan, bedside lighting, wall hooks and a luggage rack are visible in the cottage photographs.

Outlets or water leaks

Official Nespresso machine assistance
Microwave • Whirlpool WMMF7330 / WMMF7530 family
  1. Put food in a microwave-safe container.
  2. Select a cooking function or enter the time.
  3. Close the door and press START.
  4. Press CANCEL to stop.

Fan cycles through ventilation speeds; Light controls the cooktop light. Remove metal rack for ordinary microwave cooking unless the installed model's manual expressly authorizes it.

Official Whirlpool support · Find independent demonstrations
Gas range and oven

Place cookware on the grate, push and turn the burner knob counterclockwise to IGNITE, confirm ignition, adjust flame and turn OFF afterward. Keep flames within the pan edges. If ignition fails, turn off and seek assistance.

For baking: select BAKE, enter temperature, press START, wait for preheat, insert food and press CANCEL when finished. Do not line the oven floor with foil or block vents.

Official range support · Find independent range videos
Air fry

Arrange food in one layer in the basket, with a baking sheet beneath it. Select AIR FRY, temperature and START; follow the installed oven's preheat prompts. Turn food as appropriate and CANCEL when done. Exact cycle and accessories require model confirmation.

Instant Pot

Available at Narnia. Exact model and accessories are pending verification. Follow the manufacturer's manual; never force the lid open under pressure.

Find introductory Instant Pot videos
Dishwasher
  1. Scrape food and load racks.
  2. Add automatic dishwasher detergent.
  3. Check spray arms spin freely.
  4. Select cycle and press START/RESUME; close within four seconds on top-control models.

Never use hand dish soap.

Washer and dryer

Washer: add HE detergent, load loosely, power on, close door, select fabric and cycle, hold START/PAUSE three seconds. Dryer: clean lint screen, load, power on, select cycle, hold START/PAUSE until countdown completes. Do not wash or dry flammable-material-contaminated items.

Refrigerator

If equipped with Humidity Control, leave it on. Builder recommends disposing of excess ice outdoors rather than in the sink.
