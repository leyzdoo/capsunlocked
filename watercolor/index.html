YOUR TABLE, READY

Keep it simple. Leave room for water.

01 / Gather300 gsm watercolor paper, a round brush (size 6–8), a small detail brush, pencil, clean water and a mixing plate.

02 / Look, then traceOpen a study. Compare the painting with its matching outline. Print the outline and lightly transfer it to your paper with a window or graphite paper.

03 / Build slowlyBegin with diluted color, let each layer dry, and add your darkest details last. White paper is your lightest paint.

Palettes are sampled from each illustration; screen colors are visual guides, not exact pigment recipes. Use the watercolor colors you have and test a swatch first.
